# Subtitle segmentation

Use phrase boundaries and reading comfort. Default targets:

- minimum duration: 0.8 seconds unless a short reaction requires less;
- maximum duration: 6 seconds;
- maximum two lines;
- target 12 to 17 characters per second for mixed-language social clips;
- lower target for technical or dense speech;
- avoid more than 42 characters per line unless the target renderer requires it.

Keep together: names, numbers with units, negation with its verb, articles with nouns, and short English phrases used as one unit. Split at punctuation, completed clauses, or speaker turns. Never hide a word only to meet a character limit; retime or create another cue.
