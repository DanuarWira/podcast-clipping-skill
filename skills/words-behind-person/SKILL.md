# Words Behind Person

## When to Use
Use when a clip needs a large hook or keyword placed behind a speaking person or tracked subject.

Create depth-aware layers: background, text, subject mask, foreground details, and subtitles. Hook must come from transcript or approved brief. Never invent quote, number, name, or claim.

## Workflow
1. Select 1–5 source-backed words: specific claim, contrast, outcome, unusual phrase, or payoff.
2. Record timestamp, exact wording, and reason for emphasis.
3. Track subject and generate mask or rotoscope matte.
4. Place text in negative space, never across eyes, mouth, meaningful hands, subtitles, logos, or critical props.
5. Animate one restrained entrance and exit.
6. Preview full speed and paused frames; fix halo, tracking, readability, and overlap.

Preserve slang, English switches, qualifiers, and negation. If tracking confidence is low, move text beside subject or remove effect. Pass only when hook is faithful, mask is clean, text is readable, and subtitle safe zone is clear. Human-review uncertain masks and meaning-changing hooks.
