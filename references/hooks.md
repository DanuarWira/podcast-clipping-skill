# Hook patterns

Write hooks from exact source meaning. Prefer 3 to 8 words on screen, then let speech carry detail.

- Contradiction: `Everyone gets this wrong`
- Result first: `This changed his entire career`
- Specific question: `Why did he quit at 29?`
- Stakes: `One decision cost him years`
- Unpopular view: `His advice sounds wrong`
- Before and after: `From broke to profitable`
- Open loop: `The part nobody mentions`
- Direct quote: use the speaker's strongest short phrase

Reject hooks that:

- promise a fact absent from source;
- use fake urgency or invented numbers;
- remove a qualifier that changes meaning;
- reveal the entire payoff before the clip starts;
- rely on generic phrases such as `You won't believe this` without a specific reason.

Make three variants per strong candidate: curiosity, conflict, and utility. Pick one based on source tone and audience.
